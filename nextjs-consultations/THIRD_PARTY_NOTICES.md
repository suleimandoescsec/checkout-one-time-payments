# Stripe checkout example

The hosted Checkout and raw-body webhook patterns in `src/lib/booking/payments.ts`
and `src/app/api/booking/webhook/route.ts` are adapted from
[stripe-samples/checkout-one-time-payments](https://github.com/stripe-samples/checkout-one-time-payments),
forked at https://github.com/suleimandoescsec/checkout-one-time-payments.
The scheduling, dashboard, database functions and booking UI are our additions.
